07 Paper Preprint

ERASER-1: An Open-Weight Model for Complete and Verifiable Unlearning

E. Nairne1, J. Lipman2, and the ERASER Collective
1Independent  2Formerly of Hymen L. Lipman & Co.

Abstract

We present ERASER-1, an open-weight vinyl model achieving 100% unlearning accuracy on EraseBench (n=1, conducted on a single desk, possibly rigged by us). Unlike gradient-based approaches, ERASER-1 requires no retraining, no compute, and no legal department. Limitations include heavy dependency on graphite, paper, and human deployment.
